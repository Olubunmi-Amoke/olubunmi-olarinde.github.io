Ethics capstone · Clinical NLP

The Model Doesn’t Speak My Pain.

An inquiry into algorithmic bias in symptom interpretation—and what responsible AI owes to communities whose pain has historically been misunderstood.

View presentation slides Read the script
Abstract

Language is data—but it is also lived experience.

Many NLP tools used in clinical settings rely on standardized expressions of symptoms and emotion. For communities whose voices have historically been excluded or misunderstood, that design choice creates a real risk of algorithmic misinterpretation.

This work centers people living with Sickle Cell Disease and examines how participatory design, relational ethics, and epistemic justice can guide more equitable AI development.

Five lenses for more responsible systems.

Design principle

When a model interprets pain, accuracy is not the only measure that matters.

Trust, representation, agency, and the consequences of being misunderstood belong in the evaluation criteria from the beginning.
