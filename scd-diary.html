The problem

Make reflection useful—not burdensome.

People living with Sickle Cell Disease often navigate unpredictable, intense pain episodes. Traditional trackers can feel rigid, clinical, or disconnected from how people naturally describe their experiences.

The diary combines free-text reflection with light structure, helping users log in a few minutes and receive supportive, transparent insights rather than opaque scores.

Product evolution

From prediction to patient voice.

The most important design decision was recognizing when the technically obvious path was not the most responsible one.

V1 · Modeling

Predict pain and emotion

The first version explored regression and classification over diary entries, benchmarking multiple models and visualizing pain–mood patterns.

V2 · Current

Surface triggers and supportive actions

The current experience uses trigger detection and cited RAG insights to amplify what users report, offer context-aware suggestions, and preserve user agency.

Responsible-AI pivot

I re-scoped the product after recognizing that predicting symptoms for patients could minimize lived experience. The redesigned system focuses on reflection, evidence, transparency, and user control.

Approach

A modular, explainable experience.

Lightweight preprocessing, trigger detection, and retrieval work together to turn natural-language entries into clear Insight Cards.

01

Listen

Capture free-text entries plus light structure without forcing lived experience into rigid categories.

02

Ground

Retrieve from a curated SCD corpus and present supportive insights with citations and transparent controls.

03

Reflect

Offer trigger patterns, optional charts, an insight archive, and CSV export for personal or provider conversations.

Research prototype, not medical advice.

The diary supports reflection and information gathering. It does not diagnose, predict a crisis, or replace professional medical care. Participatory co-design, broader corpus coverage, multilingual support, and formal validation are important next steps.
