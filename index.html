About Me

I’m a Machine Learning Engineer and Data Scientist. I recently completed my M.S. in Data Science at Eastern University (July 2025). I build data-driven products that are effective, scalable, and, just as importantly, fair, human-centered, and ethically responsible.

I’m passionate about using data to solve real-world problems across domains such as health, education, business, and social impact. I enjoy roles that blend applied AI, research, and product development to ship practical value.

Recent work includes the Smart Sickle Cell Diary, a Streamlit app that uses LLM-assisted insights and RAG to support reflection and self-management, alongside projects in computer vision, classical ML, and analytics.

Beyond data, I’m a vocal coach and love weaving creativity into my technical work. This site features my ethics capstone, The Model Doesn’t Speak My Pain, the Smart Sickle Cell Diary (SCD) app, and selected projects.

Fun fact: I once ran a summer music camp for kids ages 6–13, equal parts chaos and pure joy!
